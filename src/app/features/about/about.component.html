01 BEFORE THE FIRST SIP

It usually starts quietly.
A little sunlight. A familiar song. The sound of a cup meeting a table.

Before anyone orders anything, a café already has a feeling. We wanted Brew & Bloom to have one of those feelings. The kind where you walk in for ten minutes and somehow end up staying for two hours.
